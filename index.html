OUR ROOTS & STORY

“Made with tradition.
Packed with love.”

Thanvi Mane (ಧನ್ಯ ಮನೆ) was born from a deep love for the comforting, unforgettable taste of pure homemade Karnataka food.

In a world dominated by commercial preservatives and mass-produced snacks, we hold fast to time-honoured culinary customs. Every item — from our crunchy hand-twisted Chakkuli and fiery spicy Kodubale to our sun-soaked Herale Kayi pickle and aromatic Hucch Ellu Pudi — is crafted in our personal kitchen following recipes passed down through generations.

We do not cut corners. We sun-dry our chillies, stone-grind our masalas, use unadulterated oils, and pack each packet with utmost hygiene as if preparing for our own children.
